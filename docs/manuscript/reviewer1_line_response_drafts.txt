Lines 142–145 (Figures 4–5) — confusion matrices and error source
Location: Section 2.4, Figures 4–5
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We agree and moved this interpretation into the main Results text (not only the caption). At the locked counting operating point (confidence ≈0.15; match IoU 0.3), cross-class assignments among matched detections total 2,600 instances (developed→aborted 826; aborted→developed 1,774) versus 35,714 true positives—substantially smaller than missed detections, background false positives, and localization-related errors. TIDE-style decomposition on test exports is consistent: miss, localization, and background dominate; class confusion accounts for a minor share (~3%). We therefore describe localization and detection quality, not pairwise class confusion, as the primary error budget.


Lines 306–308 — single-site limitation and domain adaptation
Location: Discussion, Future directions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We agree that single-site benchtop imaging of dried capitula is a major limitation for broad generalization, and we state this explicitly. We also describe a staged adaptation programme: pilot tray-targeted fine-tuning on high-error acquisition sessions (completed under a canonical test gate on pooled metrics) and, as future work, transfer from dried benchtop data (source) toward fresh material and field lighting (target), following established transfer-learning practice (we cite Yang et al., 2024, by analogy for domain-shift methodology—not as a claim that their OCT task equals ours). Leave-one-tray-out evaluation is listed as planned robustness analysis when additional trays and conditions are available.

Section 2.7 — manual validation n = 50
Location: Section 2.7
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We clarified sampling and representativeness. The n = 50 manual audit draws only from the 109-image held-out test split; train and validation images are excluded, and this subset did not influence confidence locking or model training. Sampling uses density-stratified quotas by ground-truth boxes per image (tertiles on the test pool; documented seed), with at most one image per tray key when possible.
