TECHNICAL — YOLO comparisons (v8 vs newer generations; model sizes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We thank the reviewer for raising this point. All models were evaluated on identical frozen splits using a counting-first protocol: confidence is selected on the validation split by minimizing per-image count MAE, then locked and applied unchanged on the held-out test set (n = 109 images; locked confidence ≈ 0.15). Count MAE is the primary endpoint; ranking mAP is reported separately.

The production anchor (best2.pt) achieved test count MAE 61.3 (95% CI 51.3–71.3). We trained four Ultralytics YOLO variants at medium scale for 100 epochs each (1280 px, shared augmentation, 8 GiB GPU constraint): YOLOv8m retrain MAE 111.9; YOLO11m 119.6; YOLO26m 95.3; YOLOv10m 182.0. None improved counting accuracy versus the anchor. YOLO26m illustrates the separation of metrics: test mAP50 was higher (0.41 vs 0.18) but count MAE was much worse (95.3 vs 61.3).

We have not completed a full comparison across YOLO 9 or 12 or a systematic small/medium/large grid; that remains future work. The completed comparisons address whether recent M-scale YOLO retrains outperform our anchor under fair evaluation—they do not. Our contribution is the reproducible counting-first protocol and operating-point selection, not a new architecture.

Staged tray-targeted fine-tuning on the worst acquisition sessions (post-zoo queue) improved holdout error on one tray family (350; test MAE 51.5 after stage 1) but did not provide a globally safe replacement: other trays raised pooled test MAE, so best2.pt remains the manuscript default.
