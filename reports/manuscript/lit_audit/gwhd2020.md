## 3. What the paper contributes (for HARCHOC)

- **Task:** Field **wheat head** (spike) detection in high-resolution RGB — bounding-box labels, not benchtop kernels/seeds.
- **Scale (2020 release):** **4,700** images, **~190,000** labelled heads; nine institutions, seven countries; harmonized labelling and FAIR-oriented metadata (abstract + §2).
- **Benchmark framing:** Positions dense **organ** detection under variable illumination, genotype, growth stage, motion blur, and **head overlap** — motivates duplicate/localization false positives and counting-oriented evaluation, not tray-scale two-class seed viability.
- **Competition:** Kaggle Global Wheat Head Detection (2020); paper discusses challenge organization and baseline detection metrics (mAP-style object detection on shared labels).
- **Follow-on:** GWHD 2021 (`10.34133/2021/9846158`) relabels/expands data; counting RMSE/rRMSE and competition FPR/FNR appear more explicitly in later GWC retrospectives — cite 2020 for original benchmark + overlap narrative, 2021+ for updated counts if needed.
