Comments and Suggestions for Authors

This paper addresses the recognition and counting problem of filled and aborted sunflower seeds, constructs an end-to-end detection and classification pipeline based on YOLOv8, and completes model deployment. The research targets the practical demand for high-throughput crop phenotyping and has certain application value. After careful review, the manuscript has obvious deficiencies in abstract standardization, originality, experimental design, generalization verification, research reproducibility, literature review and figure normalization. The specific comments are as follows:

    The abstract is not standardized and complete enough. It is recommended that the abstract be written in the logical framework of research purpose, research methods, experimental results and core conclusions. Key information is missing, and the abstract should be restructured and supplemented to form a complete and standardized content.
    Insufficient originality and limited academic contribution. The paper directly adopts the existing YOLOv8 model for research, and only completes dataset construction, parameter tuning and engineering deployment. It fails to propose substantive improvements targeting the characteristics of dense small sunflower seed detection in terms of network structure, feature fusion and detection algorithms. The research is mainly based on the application of existing models, lacking original algorithm innovation and academic increment. It is necessary to further refine the core contributions different from existing crop seed detection studies.
    Lack of SOTA comparison experiments. Only the YOLOv8 model is used for testing. Quantitative comparisons with mainstream state-of-the-art models in crop small-object and seed segmentation in recent years are absent, which cannot fully prove the advancement and necessity of the adopted method.
    Single experimental scenario and insufficient generalization validation. All images are collected only in a single site, single growth stage and indoor fixed lighting conditions. Field natural light, complex occlusion, different maturity levels, multiple varieties and cross-regional actual working conditions are not covered. The field robustness and generalization ability of the model are insufficiently verified, limiting the applicability of the research conclusions.
    Poor research reproducibility. Descriptions of model training parameters, threshold basis, key algorithm procedures and experimental details are too brief. Core technical parameters and implementation details are insufficiently disclosed, making it difficult for other researchers to reproduce the experiments, and the academic rigor needs to be improved.
    In-depth introduction and literature review are insufficient. The domestic and international research status of crop phenotyping, sunflower seed detection and small-object recognition is not systematically summarized. Existing research gaps are not clearly concluded, leading to an unclear research motivation and theoretical basis.
    Poor standardization and readability of figures. The font size and style of all figures are inconsistent (e.g., Figure 2 and Figure 3 use different fonts). Some figures have low resolution with overly small fonts (Figure 4, Figure 5) and blurry content (Figure 6). Some figures lack clear annotations (many functions in Figure 9 are unclear). All figures do not meet journal formatting requirements. It is suggested to carefully check and optimize all figures to improve readability and information expression.
